1. SIGN-IN AND ONBOARDING — HALF EARTH BACKDROP
====================================================================

The welcome view must show only a beautiful partial Earth behind the login form and both onboarding questions.

Required composition:

- Earth is large and positioned low/right or low/left so only approximately half of the globe enters the viewport. It should feel as if the user is looking past the edge of Earth from space, not at a centred dashboard globe.
- The login/onboarding content stays centred or slightly opposite the visible Earth edge, with ample black negative space and excellent readability.
- Earth rotates slowly and continuously in its normal base-only mode. It is an actual rendered Earth, not a CSS gradient/circle/image placeholder.
- Use natural Earth colours: realistic blue ocean, muted green/brown land, soft white ice/cloud and gentle shadow. No country-border curves, graticules, radar, CRT or bright map seams.
- Give only the outer limb a very subtle physically plausible blue atmospheric rim. The glow is thin, diffuse and calm; it must not become a neon ring, a large blue fog, a gradient background or a coloured panel.
- The star background remains sparse, dim and black. There are no decorative grids, particles or moving effects competing with the form.
- The form is a compact near-black reading surface, not a large opaque card. Keep logo, one sentence, email/password controls, one primary action and the concise demo privacy note.
- The user may drag the background Earth, but the form remains easy to use. On release, Earth resumes its gentle idle spin after the existing short delay. Reduced motion keeps it still.
- Before onboarding finishes, load no personal location, news, heatmap, live-weather or account data. This is base Earth only.

Responsive rules:

- On phone, show a larger cropped arc of Earth at the bottom or side, never behind the input fields so strongly that text becomes unreadable.
- The login form remains visually dominant and does not overlap the Earth limb awkwardly.
- Verify 1440×900, 1024×768 and 375×812 explicitly.

2. MAIN PAGE — SUBTLE BLUE ATMOSPHERE ONLY
====================================================================

Keep Main predominantly black, white and grey. Add only a restrained blue atmospheric glow around the Earth:

- The glow follows the Earth’s circular limb rather than the whole page, side panels or controls.
- It is faint enough that a user notices atmosphere/depth, not a colour effect. Avoid blue gradients across the page.
- It uses one shared design token, so its alpha and hue match Welcome and Main exactly.
- It must remain behind data layers, markers and callouts. It cannot wash out heatmaps, reduce text contrast or cause a mismatched shade.
- No other blue interface accents, coloured boxes, blue borders or decorative shadows are introduced.

3. FIX NEWS POPUPS — CLEAN ROTATING GLOBAL-CITY STORY CYCLE
====================================================================

Current popup behaviour is not acceptable. Implement a clearly visible, elegant automatic sequence of news callouts that moves location by location as Earth rotates.

Authorised city set:

- Add a small fixed, documented global showcase set of main cities across continents. Use approximately 8–12 cities, for example London, New York, São Paulo, Cairo, Delhi, Singapore, Tokyo, Sydney, Johannesburg and Mexico City.
- This limited city list is intentional. Do not fetch arbitrary worldwide coordinates or create unbounded API work.
- Saved user locations take priority. When a saved location is visible and has news, include it before or alongside the showcase cities.
- Fetch/cache city news through the existing safe backend news flow. Respect provider rate limits, cache/stale state, publisher verification and failure fallbacks. Do not block Main render while news loads.

Visual/interaction behaviour:

1. Earth spins normally.
2. When a city is on the visible hemisphere and has a story, show one small location marker on the exact projected city location.
3. Animate a thin leader line outward from the surface point, following the apparent outward direction from the curved Earth.
4. Animate one compact callout into place at the end of that leader: opacity and a small 6–12 px outward translation over roughly 180–250 ms. No bounce, flash, pulse or large scaling.
5. Callout shows only:
   - severity glyph;
   - one/two-line headline;
   - publisher and date.
6. After a calm dwell, fade/contract it, then reveal the next eligible city when visible. The globe continues rotating throughout.

Cleanliness rules:

- Show at most one automatic popup at any time. Never show a side list, a popup forest or a stack of cards.
- Keep callouts within the stage and away from left controls, timeline, account control and edges.
- As a city approaches the limb or rotates behind Earth, fade/tuck its marker, leader and callout before it looks detached. Re-project all three continuously with globe movement; they must never drift or point to the wrong place.
- User hover, keyboard focus, click, globe drag, timeline scrubbing or an open drawer takes control immediately and pauses/defer the automatic cycle. Resume only after the interaction is over and the short idle delay has elapsed.
- With reduced motion, do not auto-cycle. Expose the same news through keyboard-focusable markers and a concise manual next/previous story control if needed.

Severity and trust:

- Verified publisher: green outline/glyph for low attention, amber for medium attention, red for high attention. The callout background stays near-black/white; colour belongs only to the marker, leader and glyph.
- Unverified publisher: neutral grey dashed marker/leader, no urgency colour and the visible label Unverified news context.
- In expanded detail, always state: News context, not scientific evidence or an official warning.
- Do not fabricate stories, severity, publisher, locations or dates. On no-news/provider-error states, show no automatic popup and retain a concise honest state in the manual detail path.

Implementation expectation:

- Replace any current “up to three automatic callouts” selection with explicit single-active-callout state, deterministic queue/cooldown and cleanup on visibility/interaction changes.
- Keep the queue bounded and stable: do not let animation frames trigger fetches, duplicate timers or state churn.
- Tests must cover queue selection, only-one-active invariant, visible/hidden/limb behaviour, user takeover, reduced motion and verified/unverified visual mapping.
